# Operations and resilience doctrine

- CDD-OPS-001: Queue, worker, pool, lock, retry, timeout, and concurrency changes require failure-path evidence.
- CDD-OPS-002: Production/destructive actions are outside autonomous implementation unless explicitly approved.
- CDD-OPS-003: Stress and soak evidence is selected by actual resource or long-running impact, not ceremony.
- CDD-OPS-004: Rollback must preserve contract and evidence history.
